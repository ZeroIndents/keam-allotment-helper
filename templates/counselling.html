The allotment journey, step by step

Exact dates change every year — always check cee.kerala.gov.in for the current schedule. This is the shape of the process.

1 · Rank list published
CEE publishes the State Merit rank list. Check your rank and category (SM / EZ / MU / SC …).
2 · Option registration opens
Log in to the candidate portal and fill your choice list — every college + course combo you'd accept, in order of preference.
3 · Confirm options before each round
Before Round 2 and Round 3 you must re-confirm your options on the portal, or you're out of that round.
4 · Round 1 allotment → report & pay
If allotted, report to the college and pay fees within the deadline. Missing it cancels the seat.
5 · Rounds 2 & 3 — possible upgrades
Participating in later rounds can upgrade you to a better option. Your lower options are dropped automatically once you're allotted.
6 · Provisional & mop-up rounds
Final rounds fill the remaining seats. After this, admission is confirmed — complete reporting and document verification.

Rules that save your seat

Order matters

You get the highest option you qualify for. Options below your allotment are wiped automatically — always list what you truly want, first.

Deadlines are final

Fee payment + college reporting deadlines are strict. Missing one cancels your seat and removes you from later rounds.

Re-confirm every round

Participating in Round 2/3 requires you to confirm your options again on the portal before each round's deadline.

Upgrades are real

Cutoffs usually relax in later rounds. Keeping your list live means you can move up to a better college automatically.

Guide only — official rules & dates are at cee.kerala.gov.in
