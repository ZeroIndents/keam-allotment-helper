🤔 First time here? How to read this page (click to open)

• Rank = your position in the merit list. A smaller number is better (rank 1 = topper).

• Opening rank = the rank of the best student who got a seat in that college + course.

• Cutoff rank (closing rank) = the rank of the last student who got a seat. If your rank is better (smaller) than the cutoff, you had a good chance of getting in.

• SM (State Merit) = the open / general category. Other letters (EZ, MU, SC…) are reservation quotas — each has its own cutoff.

• Rounds (Phase 1, 2, 3) = allotments happen in stages. Students switch seats between rounds, which is why cutoffs move.

• Everything here is from past official allotment lists — use it to judge your chances, not as a guarantee.

      📖 Terms & category codes explained (click to open)

      Common terms

      • Rank — your position on the merit list. Smaller = better.

      • Opening rank — rank of the best student admitted to a college + course.

      • Cutoff (closing) rank — rank of the last student admitted. Beat this to have gotten in.

      • SM (State Merit) — the open / general category, no reservation.

      Some quota categories

      EZ — Ezhava · MU — Muslim · LA — Latin Catholic · BH — Backward Hindu · SC — Scheduled Caste · ST — Scheduled Tribe · EW — Economically Weaker · OEC — Other Eligible Community · GN — General
