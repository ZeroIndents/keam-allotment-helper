How should I fill my options? (Strategy guide)

🧠 The 3-bucket strategy (Dream → Safe → Backup)

Order your choice list strictly by preference, not by guess. CEE allots you the highest option you qualify for — so put the college you want most first, even if it feels ambitious.

  • Dream options — the ambitious ones (top government colleges / CSE). If you qualify, amazing. If not, you simply fall to your next choice.
  • Realistic options — where your rank comfortably matches last year's cutoffs (your Moderate picks).
  • Backup / sure-shot options — your Safe picks, so you always land a seat.

⚠️ CEE rules that cost students their seats

  • Options below your allotment are wiped automatically. Once you're allotted a college, every option below it disappears — so order matters.
  • Confirm your options before every round you want to participate in (the portal asks you to re-confirm between rounds).
  • Report to the allotted college and pay fees within the deadline — missing it cancels your seat and you lose later rounds too.
  • You can be upgraded in a later round if a better option opens up — keep your list live.
