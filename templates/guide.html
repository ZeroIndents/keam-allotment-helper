Full step-by-step guides

1 · Predict your rank

What it does:

Your final KEAM rank comes from two parts: your entrance exam score (out of 300) plus your Class 12 marks (converted to a score out of 300) using the official 5:3:2 formula — Math counts 50%, Physics 30%, Chemistry 20%. This tool does that math for you and shows an expected rank range.

Step by step:

  1. Open the Predict Rank page (button on the homepage, or the card above).
  2. Choose Raw score / 600 if you remember your entrance marks out of 600. If you already know CEE's normalized score out of 300, switch to Normalized / 300.
  3. Select your Class 12 board (Kerala State DHSE or CBSE/ISC).
  4. Enter your Math, Physics and Chemistry board marks.
  5. Watch the live meter at the bottom — it previews your total as you type.
  6. Press Calculate Expected Rank. You'll get a colored rank range (e.g. "3,000 - 4,000") and a plain-language explanation.
  7. Use the compare panel to see real 2026 cutoffs for CS/EC/ME/EE/CE and whether they're Within reach, Borderline or Tougher.
Heads up: the entrance score is normalized by CEE based on how candidates performed that year, so the halving is an estimate. Treat the result as a guide, not a guarantee.
2 · Find your options

What it does:

The most asked question in Kerala every year: "With my rank, which college can I get?" This tool compares your rank against the real closing ranks of every college-course combination in the database and tags each one.

Step by step:

  1. Open the Find My Options page.
  2. Enter your rank (smaller is better — rank 1 is the top student).
  3. Pick your category (usually SM — State Merit) and round (Phase 3, the final round, is the most useful).
  4. Optionally type a course code (CS, EC, ME...) to narrow it down. Leave blank to see everything.
  5. Press Go — you'll get a table of colleges with verdicts.

What the colors mean:

  • Safe — last year's cutoff was comfortably below your rank. Very likely in.
  • Moderate — your rank is right around last year's cutoff. 50-50.
  • Ambitious — just missed it last year, but cutoffs move every year.
Strategy tip: order your CEE choice list by preference, not by safety — include your dream college first, then realistic ones, then a sure-shot backup. CEE gives you the highest option you qualify for.
3 · Search cutoffs & allotments (homepage)

What it does:

The homepage is a giant, searchable table of the official allotment lists — every student who was allotted a seat, with their register number, rank, college, course, category and seat type.

Ways to search:

  • By register number — type a Reg No to see exactly where that student was allotted.
  • By rank — see which colleges students around your rank were admitted to.
  • By college / course / category / seat — browse the filters to explore cutoffs (e.g. filter college + course to see opening and closing ranks per category).
  • Use Year (2026/2025/2024) and Round (Phase 1/2/3) to compare how things changed.

Pro trick:

Select a college and a course together and the page shows Category Rank Ranges cards above the table — the opening → closing rank for each category. That's the exact "cutoff" everyone talks about.

4 · Read the statistics

What it does:

A dashboard for spotting patterns — written in plain language so you don't need a data degree to read it.

What each section means:

  • Quick Takeaways — the page's auto-generated highlights (toughest branch, easiest to enter, where competition is fierce).
  • Toughest Branches — courses with the lowest cutoffs (hardest to get into).
  • How cutoffs moved round by round — did cutoffs rise or fall between Phase 1 → 2 → 3? Falling = students moving up, freeing seats.
  • Students who switched colleges — how many people jumped colleges between rounds, and where they went.
  • How much do cutoffs swing? — the gap between opening and closing rank for each college. Big swing = lots of students admitted.
  • Compare two colleges — side-by-side cutoffs to help you decide between two picks.
  • Heatmap — a color grid of how tough each college is per course (darker = tougher).
Start with the "How to read this page" guide at the top of the statistics page — it explains rank, opening vs closing, and quota codes in one sentence each.
5 · Prepare for counselling

What it does:

Two things students always stress about: "When is everything happening?" and "What documents do I need?" This page answers both.

Step by step:

  1. Open the Counselling Guide page.
  2. Read the timeline — the shape of the process from option registration to final round. (Exact dates change yearly; always confirm on cee.kerala.gov.in.)
  3. Work through the document checklist — tick items off as you gather them.
  4. Your progress (e.g. "8 / 12 ready") is saved on your device — close the page and come back later, it remembers.
Don't skip: reporting to the allotted college and paying fees within the deadline. Missing it cancels your seat.
6 · Resize your photo & signature

What it does:

Application portals reject photos that are the wrong size. This tool crops and resizes your photo or signature to the exact pixel size you need — right in your browser, nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Step by step:

  1. Open the Resize Files page.
  2. Upload your photo (or signature).
  3. Pick the target size (e.g. 100×120 px), or choose a preset.
  4. Crop the image in the preview, then download the resized file.

Plain-language glossary

Words you'll see everywhere on this site — decoded.

Rank

Your position among all KEAM candidates. Rank 1 is the best. Smaller number = better.

Opening rank (Topper)

The rank of the best student admitted to that college & course. The "front door" of the cutoff.

Closing rank (Cutoff / Last admitted)

The rank of the last student who got a seat. If your rank is better (smaller) than the closing rank, you would have been admitted.

Phase / Round

CEE allots seats in rounds: Phase 1, then 2, then 3. Cutoffs usually change between rounds as students upgrade and free up seats.

SM (State Merit)

The general category — no reservation. Most students fall here. The default choice on this site.

Quota codes (EZ, MU, SC, ST...)

Reserved categories — e.g. EZ = Ezhava, MU = Muslim, SC = Scheduled Caste, ST = Scheduled Tribe, EW = Economically Weaker. Cutoffs differ per category; the full list is in the legend on the Statistics page.

5:3:2 formula

How board marks are weighted for KEAM: Math 50% + Physics 30% + Chemistry 20%. That's the "5:3:2" you keep hearing.

Index / Total index

Your final combined score out of 600 (board part + entrance part) that decides your rank.

Common questions

Is this data official?

The underlying data comes from CEE Kerala's official allotment PDFs. This site makes them searchable and readable — but always verify final results on cee.kerala.gov.in.

Is my rank prediction accurate?

It's a well-grounded estimate based on the official formula and 2026 trend data. Actual ranks depend on how everyone performs that year, so treat ranges as guidance.

Do cutoffs from last year guarantee my admission this year?

No — cutoffs move every year with competition, seats and difficulty. That's why the options tool shows Safe / Moderate / Ambitious instead of a single yes/no.

Does the site collect my personal data?

Nothing identifiable. Your theme choice and checklist progress stay on your own device, and the site only records anonymous page visits to improve itself.
