How is my rank calculated?

Step 1 — Board marks (max 300): Each subject's marks are converted to a percentage, then weighted 50% Math + 30% Physics + 20% Chemistry (the official 5:3:2 ratio) and scaled to a maximum of 300.

Step 2 — Add your KEAM score (max 300).

Step 3 — Total index (max 600) is matched against 2026 Brilliant Pala rank brackets to estimate your State Merit rank range. Actual ranks also depend on tie-breakers and how all candidates perform, so treat this as a guide.
